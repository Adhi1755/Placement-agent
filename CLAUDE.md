## Design Context
- **Register**: brand (landing) + product (app/dashboard)
- **Purpose**: turn a résumé + JD into a credible "hiring committee" verdict and a plan to improve
- **Audience**: students / early-career engineers prepping for placements — anxious, outcome-focused, laptop + phone
- **Personality**: editorial, judicial, weighty
- **DNA**: "The Verdict" — Editorial Tribunal (warm paper, ink, oxblood seal, serif display) — see DESIGN.md
- **Constraints**: Next.js 16 (App Router), Tailwind v4, Framer Motion, WCAG AA, responsive
- **Hard nos**: dark-mode-default, cyan-on-dark, purple→blue gradients, glassmorphism, identical card grids, centered-everything, Geist/Inter as primary
